Privacy Policy

Application: Universal TV Remote

Developer: Deep Patel

Effective date: November 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Universal TV Remote ("the App") is a mobile application that allows users to discover, pair with, and control compatible smart TVs over a local Wi-Fi network.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, used, stored, and shared when you use the App.

2. Information Used by the App

Local Network Information

The App uses local network access to discover compatible TVs and communicate with them.

This may involve accessing:

  • Local IP addresses of compatible TVs
  • TV device names and identifiers
  • Network discovery information
  • Pairing and connection status

This information is used to provide TV discovery, pairing, reconnection, and remote control functionality.

Saved TV Information

The App may store information about previously connected TVs on your device, including device names, network addresses, device identifiers, and connection preferences.

This information helps users reconnect to their TVs without repeating the setup process.

3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP addresses
  • Device and app information
  • Ad interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information

This information may be used for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, ad personalization.

For more information, visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Privacy Information

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable laws and advertising policies, the App requests consent or provides privacy choices through Google's consent management technology.

Users in applicable regions may be able to accept, reject, or manage advertising-related data processing.

Where required, users can revisit their choices through the App's Settings screen.

Declining advertising consent does not prevent users from accessing the App's core TV remote functionality.

5. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by the App is used to:

  • Discover compatible TVs
  • Establish and maintain TV connections
  • Send remote control commands
  • Remember previously connected devices
  • Display and measure advertisements
  • Maintain app functionality and security

6. Information Sharing

The App communicates with compatible TVs on the user's local network to provide remote control functionality.

The App also integrates Google AdMob, which may process information as described in Section 3.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Saved TV information and connection preferences may be retained on the user's device until removed through the App, cleared through device settings, or deleted when the App is uninstalled, subject to operating system behavior and backups.

Google AdMob may retain information under Google's own policies.

Users can remove saved TVs using the App's device-management features.

8. Data Security

The App uses supported operating system and network technologies to communicate with compatible TVs and manage locally stored information.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed for children.

The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated backend.

Google AdMob advertising settings and applicable child-directed treatment requirements will be configured according to the App's intended audience and applicable laws.

10. International Users

The App may be available in multiple countries. Privacy rights and advertising consent requirements may vary by location.

Users may contact the developer regarding privacy questions or applicable data protection rights.
